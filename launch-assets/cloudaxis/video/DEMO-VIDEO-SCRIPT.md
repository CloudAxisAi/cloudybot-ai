**Goal:** Show outcome, not features. Viewer should think: "I describe work once, it runs while I sleep."

**Tool:** OBS, Loom, or Screen Studio · **Resolution:** 1920×1080 · **Audio:** your voice narrating

## Recording tips

- Hide personal email in top bar (use blank demo account `info@cloudybot.ai` if cleaner).
- Pause notifications on your machine.
- Use **dark mode** (default) — matches brand.
- Export **MP4 H.264**, under 50 MB for Product Hunt.
- Save as `video/cloudaxis-demo-90s.mp4` in this folder when done.
